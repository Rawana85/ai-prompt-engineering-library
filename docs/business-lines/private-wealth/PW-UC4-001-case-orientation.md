# PW-UC4-001 – Case Orientation Assistant

## Business Area
Private Wealth

## Status
Proposed

## Capability
Summarisation
Information Extraction

## Intent

Support case officers to build an initial understanding of the taxpayer or group by consolidating relevant profile, compliance and case history information into a structured case summary.

The objective is to establish a consistent approach for sourcing, exporting and supplying information to Copilot to improve consistency, repeatability and quality.

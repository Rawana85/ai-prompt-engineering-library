# PW-UC4-001 – Case Orientation Assistant

## Business Area
Private Wealth

## Status
Proposed

## Capability
Summarisation
Information Extraction

## Intent

Support case officers to build an initial understanding of the taxpayer or group by consolidating relevant profile, compliance and case history information into a structured case summary.

The objective is to establish a consistent approach for sourcing, exporting and supplying information to Copilot to improve consistency, repeatability and quality.

## Inputs

- CCD
- Enterprise Client Profile (ECP)
- Siebel
- Integrated Core Processing (ICP)
- Profiling Dashboard Reports
- Group Structure Information

## Expected Outcome

Generate a structured case overview including:

- Taxpayer and group profile
- Client type and age
- Business activities and industry
- Key contacts
- Related entities
- Wealth information
- Ownership percentages
- Compliance history
- Previous interactions
- Previous outcomes
- Outstanding lodgments
- Initial risk indicators

## Benefits

- Faster case familiarisation
- Consistent case summaries
- Reduced manual effort

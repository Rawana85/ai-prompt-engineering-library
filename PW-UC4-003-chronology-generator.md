# PW-UC4-003 – Case Chronology Generator

## Business Area

Private Wealth

## Status

Proposed

## Capability

- Event Extraction
- Timeline Generation
- Information Synthesis

## Intent

Support case officers to organise events, transactions and documents into a coherent timeline.

The objective is to improve case understanding and reduce effort required to manually construct chronologies.

## Inputs

ATO Information

- System extracts
- Compliance history

Taxpayer Information

- Correspondence
- Contracts
- Agreements
- Loan documents
- Trust deeds
- Resolutions
- Financial statements
- Asset transactions
- Meeting minutes
- Legal documents

## Expected Outcome

Chronology including:

- Event date
- Event description
- Relevant parties
- Reference documents

## Benefits

- Easier case reconstruction
- Faster investigations
- Improved understanding of events
